A website concept · Responding to the 2026 RFP

Move the dialfor women in tourism.

WORTH Association educates, elevates and engages women across recreation, tourism and hospitality. Their RFP asks for a digital presence that is more engaging, more accessible and more resource-rich. This concept shows what that could look like — and how we would build it.

Programs

Three pillars, one community.

Everything WORTH Association does maps to educate, elevate and engage. A redesign should make that structure the navigation — so every visitor finds their door in one click.

01

WORTH Academy

Professional development that builds leadership confidence — including the flagship 6-week program for women advancing their careers.

Educate 02

Mentorship

Emerging leaders matched with experienced industry professionals who understand the sector's challenges and opportunities.

Educate · Engage 03

Leadership Summit

The annual gathering — 2025's theme: “All Ages, All Stages: Building the Future Together” — plus WORTH-shops and networking events.

Engage 04

Scholarships

Financial support that breaks down barriers to professional development and leadership opportunities.

Elevate 05

Advocacy & Research

Data-driven advocacy for gender equity across the visitor economy, aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Stay connected

The newsletter is the community's front door.

In the concept, signup sits one scroll from the hero — name, email, consent, done. No dark patterns, no pre-ticked boxes.

Program updates, event invites, mentorship calls. Unsubscribe anytime.

Demo form. In this concept build nothing is sent anywhere — a production site would connect this to WORTH Association's email platform.
