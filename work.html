Design standards

The bar we'd hold your site to.

Pulled from our master design practice — the same checklist behind every build we ship.

Type & layout

Editorial discipline

One display voice, measured line lengths, deliberate whitespace. Headlines hand-broken, never auto-wrapped into awkward breaks.

Imagery

Real faces, real rooms

Your summit photos, your mentors, your community — never stock strangers pretending to be members. Licensed or owned assets only.

Motion

Quiet confidence

One signature moment per page, purposeful transitions, full reduced-motion support. Motion explains; it never decorates.

Content

Written to be read

Short pages that say one thing well beat long pages that say everything badly. SEO and AI-search structure live underneath, invisibly.

Trust

Sourced everything

Impact numbers with sources and years. Partner logos only with permission. Policies in plain language.

Craft

Sweat the details

Focus rings, form states, empty states, 404s, selection color. The details are where “professional” is decided.
