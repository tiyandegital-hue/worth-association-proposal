Process

From RFPto launch.

An indicative 10-week arc for a project like WORTH Association's rebuild. The formal proposal would fix dates against your calendar — the RFP deadline is November 20, 2026.

01
Weeks 1–2

Discovery & content

Stakeholder interviews, content audit, analytics and accessibility baseline. We agree the sitemap, the CMS choice and what success looks like.

02
Weeks 3–5

Design

Design system, homepage and key templates, reviewed with your team in clickable prototypes. Two structured revision rounds — no endless loops.

03
Weeks 6–8

Build

Semantic, accessible, performant code. CMS integration, forms, newsletter, events and resource library wired up and tested.

04
Week 9

QA & accessibility audit

Cross-device testing, WCAG 2.2 AA audit with fixes, performance budget sign-off, content entry and team training.

05
Week 10

Launch & handover

Staged launch, analytics and search setup, documentation, and a care plan so the site stays fast and current.

What we'd need from WORTH Association
  • Brand assets — logo files, colors, fonts
  • Real photography — summits, mentors, community
  • Program copy — or we draft, you approve
  • One decision-maker for design reviews
  • Hosting/DNS access at launch week
The RFP

November 20, 2026

Proposals go to paige@worthassociation.com (per WORTH Association's LinkedIn post, Oct 30, 2026). We will submit a formal, fixed-quote proposal by the deadline — this concept is the preview, not the submission.
