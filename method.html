Our method · Tiyan Digital

Psychology first,then design, then code.

This is the process behind the concept — and the process we would bring to WORTH Association's rebuild. No generic templates, no invented claims, no accessibility afterthoughts.

01
Discovery

Listen before we draw

Content audit of the current site, stakeholder interviews, analytics review, and an accessibility baseline. We map every audience — members, mentors, sponsors, press — to the one action each needs to take.

02
Strategy

Psychology → design → code

We define what each visitor must feel, trust and do. Then the design system: type voice, color position, layout idea, one signature moment per page. Only then do we write code.

03
Design

Editorial, warm, assured

Association sites win with clarity and humanity: real stories, honest numbers with sources, generous whitespace. The RFP's three asks — engaging, accessible, resource-rich — become design requirements, not adjectives.

04
Build

Accessible as standard, not as add-on

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard support, semantic HTML, visible focus, reduced-motion support, plain-language copy. Performance budget: fast on a rural phone connection, not just downtown wifi.

05
Handover

A site your team can actually run

We hand over a CMS the communications team can update without a developer — with training, documentation, and a care plan for the months after launch.

Non-negotiables

What every build ships with.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA

Contrast-verified palette, keyboard-navigable everything, screen-reader labels, captions on media, reduced-motion path. Checked, not claimed.

Honesty

No invented proof

Every statistic carries a named source and year. No stock-photo testimonials, no fabricated reviews, no “#1” claims without basis.

Performance

Fast everywhere

Optimized media, system fonts, no bloat. A donor on a phone in a small town gets the same experience as a sponsor on fiber.

Ownership

Yours, fully

Domain in your name, code and content you own outright, portable to any host. No lock-in, ever.

Privacy

Plain-language policies

Privacy, terms and cookie policies written for humans. Forms ask only for what's needed, with explicit consent.

Local law

Canadian context

PIPEDA-aware data handling, CASL-compliant email consent, bilingual-ready structure for EN/FR content.

The method is the pitch.

We would rather show you a concept than send you a brochure. The formal proposal — scope, timeline, fixed quote — follows the same discipline.
